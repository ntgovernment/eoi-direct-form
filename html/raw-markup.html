Direct publish an EOI

  • Link
  • MS Word Document

Fill in this form to publish an EOI for internal vacancies of 6 months or less.

This form is for Workforce Services only and will publish straight away without going through workflow.

EOIs can be published on:

  • the agency intranet, or
  • both the agency intranet and NTG Central.

Before you submit, you must upload the job description.

Enter the ?rtfid=VALUE to have a Apply Now button.
Hold the 'CTRL' key and click to select multiple designations.
Please select at least one designation.
An EOI must be advertised for at least 3 business days. The closing date may change depending on when it’s published.

The selected close date is less than 3 business days from today. An EOI must be advertised for at least 3 business days.
Use 1 January 2026 to 1 May 2026 or you can use ASAP to 1 May 2026.
Do not use abbreviated dates (01/01/26) or “Fixed for”.
Please select at least one location.
The EOI will be advertised on the agency intranet and NTG Central.
Untick NTG Central to advertise only on the agency intranet.
